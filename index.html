What is Tumaini Centre?

Where Faith Meets Professional Excellence

Tumaini — meaning hope in Swahili — is more than a name. It is the promise at the heart of everything we do. Nestled on Elgeyo Marakwet Road in Kilimani, Tumaini Centre is a purpose-built retreat and professional hub operated by the Association of Sisterhoods of Kenya (AOSK), one of the country's most enduring faith-based institutions.

Whether you are a corporate executive seeking a tranquil base away from Nairobi's bustle, an NGO coordinating a multi-day workshop, a religious delegation visiting from abroad, or a professional looking to upgrade your digital skills — Tumaini Centre was built for you. Our facility blends the serenity of a spiritual retreat with the infrastructure of a modern professional centre: fast internet, full catering, secretarial support, and a purpose-built computer training lab that has quietly shaped Kenya's ICT landscape since 2008.

We sit just minutes from Yaya Centre and the Nairobi CBD via Ngong Road, yet our green, walled campus feels a world removed from city noise. That contrast — accessible yet serene — is what keeps our guests returning.

The Association of Sisterhoods of Kenya

A Legacy of Faith, Service & Sisterhood Since 1962.

Institutional Heritage

Who We Are

The Association of Sisterhoods of Kenya (AOSK) is one of the oldest and most respected faith-based organisations in East Africa — a unified body that has shaped education, healthcare, social welfare, and community development across Kenya for more than six decades.

Founded in 1962 and formally registered as a corporate body under the Perpetual Succession Act, AOSK serves as the coordinating umbrella for Catholic Women's Religious Congregations throughout Kenya. With membership spanning over 160 congregations representing nearly 6,000 sisters, the association channels the collective strength of a vast sisterhood towards tangible social transformation.

AOSK works at the intersection of faith and society — advocating for justice, promoting holistic formation of religious women, and partnering with the Kenya Episcopal Conference (KEC) to address the evolving needs of Kenyan communities. Tumaini Centre is the physical expression of this mission in Nairobi: a space built to host, train, equip, and inspire.

Our Name, Our Promise

Why "Tumaini"?

Tumaini is the Swahili word for hope. It is the defining word of this centre's identity — and no name could be more fitting for a facility born from the vision of thousands of women who have dedicated their lives to serving others.

Every guest who walks through our gates — whether arriving for a three-day board retreat, a digital skills course, or a quiet overnight stay — arrives at a place built on the belief that human beings flourish when they are cared for, equipped, and surrounded by peace.

That is Tumaini. That is what we offer.

Vision & Mission

What Guides Us

Our Vision

"To be an association of Prophetic religious women united in Christ witnessing love and hope for the transformation of society."

This vision shapes every decision we make — from how we welcome guests at Tumaini Centre to how we engage with national policy on justice, peace, and the environment.

Our Mission

To respond to the needs of Religious women for solid holistic formation, education, and skills for ministry and leadership — while speaking with a unified voice on issues related to justice, peace, and integrity of creation.

Tumaini Centre operationalises this mission by providing a world-class environment where formation, education, and rest coexist.

Threefold Aim

Our Guiding Commitments

AOSK was established around three interlocking commitments that remain as relevant today as they were in 1962:

  • Spiritual Welfare — Promote the spiritual welfare and holistic development of Religious Women in Kenya, supporting their formation, mental wellbeing, and continued growth in ministry.
  • Unity & Co-operation — Foster communication and collaboration among member institutes, respecting each congregation's autonomy, unique charism, and spiritual tradition.
  • National Partnership — Promote active collaboration with the Kenya Episcopal Conference (KEC) to identify and respond to the social, spiritual, and developmental needs of the people of Kenya.

ICT Training Lab — Established 2008

Shaping Kenya's Digital Workforce Since 2008

In a country where digital literacy is increasingly the dividing line between opportunity and exclusion, the AOSK Computer Training Lab has quietly and consistently done the work that matters — equipping ordinary Kenyans with extraordinary skills.

Since its establishment in 2008, the A.O.S.K Computer Lab has trained hundreds of students from across Nairobi in practical, market-relevant digital skills. Our programmes range from foundational computer literacy — ideal for first-time users — to advanced courses in graphic design, web development, and computerised accounting using industry-standard platforms like QuickBooks, Sage, and Pastel.

Training is delivered by highly qualified and experienced professionals who bring both technical expertise and genuine commitment to learner success. Class sizes are intentionally kept manageable, ensuring every student receives the attention they need to truly master new skills — not just pass a test.

Whether you are a school leaver looking for your first job, a professional seeking to upskill, a business owner wanting to modernise your accounts, or a corporate training manager seeking an external facility — the A.O.S.K Computer Lab has a programme for you.

Our Approach

Practical Skills. Real-World Outcomes.

We do not teach technology for its own sake. Every module in our curriculum is designed around a practical question: How will this skill help you in work or in life? From typing your first document in Microsoft Word to filing digital accounts in QuickBooks — every lesson connects directly to a real-world application.

Our students graduate with the confidence to walk into an office, open a business, or pursue further technical education. Many of our alumni have gone on to careers in accounting, graphic design, digital communications, and web development — right here in Nairobi.

The lab is also available for private hire by corporates, training institutions, and NGOs who need a dedicated, fully equipped technology training environment.
